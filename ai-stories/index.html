AI Stories Uploader

AI Stories Uploader is a personal tool built by Trax Palmer. It helps him upload his own original, AI-generated story Shorts to his own YouTube channel.

What this app does

  • Connects only to Trax’s own YouTube account (with his permission).
  • Uploads videos he created for his AI Stories channel.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API youtube.upload scope only.
  • Does not provide uploads for other people’s channels.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for how OAuth tokens are stored (on the owner’s machine only) and how to revoke access.
